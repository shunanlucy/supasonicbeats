# 🎛️ MUSIC PRODUCTION PLAYBOOK — HIT FORMULA & SOUND DESIGN RULES

This document serves as the standard operating procedure (SOP) for producing, arranging, and mastering tracks for Supasonic Beats to achieve viral watch time and audiophile-grade car bass.

---

## 📐 Golden Audio Benchmarks (The Hit Recipe)

1. **Tempo (BPM): 120 – 124 BPM**
   - 120 BPM: Deep, sensual, romantic late-night drive mood (`AAO NA`, `MAJBOOR`).
   - 124 BPM: High-energy club banger, festival dancefloor bounce (`SAJNA`, `DEEDAR DE`).

2. **The 18–22 Second Drop Rule:**
   - **Critical Rule:** Never let the first beat drop later than **22 seconds**.
   - YouTube audience bounce rate drops by 35% if the listener hears a vocal earworm hook in the first 0–10 seconds and the main 808 sub-bass drop hits before 0:22.
   - If Suno generates a 35s atmospheric intro, immediately trim the front section to bring the drop into the 18–22s pocket (just like we did for `SAJNA`).

3. **Sub-Bass Tuning (The 40–70Hz Target):**
   - Competitor sub-bass energy sits at **60%–62%** of total spectrum.
   - Use an 808 sub-bass or pure sine sub layer centered around **45Hz – 55Hz**.
   - Sidechain the kick drum to the sub-bass with a fast release (50ms) so the kick punch (90–120Hz) cuts through without muddying the sub rumble.

4. **Vocal Production & Duet Dynamic:**
   - **Vocal Presence Target:** 7% – 10% spectral energy in the 1kHz – 4kHz range.
   - Male vocal: Warm low-mids (250Hz), gentle dip at 500Hz, presence at 3kHz.
   - Female vocal: High-shelf air boost at 10kHz, smooth de-essing at 6kHz.
   - Male-Female interplay: Male sets the groove/question, female delivers the emotional climax/hook.

5. **Mastering Loudness Standards:**
   - **Integrated Loudness:** **-14.0 to -13.5 LUFS**
   - **True Peak:** **-0.5 to 0.0 dBFS** (Never exceed +0.2 dBFS to prevent YouTube normalization distortion).
   - **Loudness Range (LRA):** 5.0 to 7.5 LU (Dynamic enough for drop impact, tight enough for car subwoofers).

## 🧠 Suno AI Prompt Engineering Architecture

To generate hits that sound organic, emotional, and commercially polished:

### Core Prompt Structure:
```text
[Genre]: Bollywood Afro House, Deep House, Melodic Club Groove
[Tempo/Key]: 124 BPM, D minor, rolling groove
[Vocals]: Seductive male and female romantic duet, emotive Indian vocal phrasing, resonant chest voice, passionate harmonized chorus
[Instruments]: Warm rolling 808 sub-bass, punchy 4-on-the-floor kick, organic afro shakers, wooden rimshots, lush atmospheric pad, subtle plucky synth lead
[Arrangement]: [Intro with vocal hook] -> [Fast Build] -> [Massive 124 BPM Drop] -> [Duet Verse] -> [Second Drop] -> [Outro]
```

### Lyric Engineering Rules:
- Blend short, memorable Hindi/Urdu keywords (*Sajna, Nazrein, Majboor, Raat, Fitoor, Saheba, Deedar*).
- Simple rhyming structure with high phonetic resonance (aa, ee, oo sounds sustain best over rolling sub-bass).
- Never write overly complex or lengthy poetic verses — Afro House needs **repetitive, hypnotic earworm loops**.
